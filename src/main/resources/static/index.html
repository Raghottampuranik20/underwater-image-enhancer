What happens to your photo

1

White balance

Removes the blue-green cast water leaves on everything.

2

Red recovery

Restores red content, the wavelength water absorbs first.

3

Local contrast

Recovers detail hiding in murky, low-contrast patches.

4

Brightness

Lifts midtones that read dark and flat underwater.

5

Sharpen

Brings back edges softened by scattering.
